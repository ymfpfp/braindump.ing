## During

Notes on learning Japanese.

- https://www.tofugu.com/learn-japanese/?curius=3118 - THE BIG GUIDE
- 32 kanji per week/100 vocab words with those kanji and the previous kanji per week
- there needs to be a tofugu for every language, i'm not kidding
- learning hiragana
  - campanas de japanese
  - tofugu is VERY good
- kanji is fucking annoying and crazy

* words are made up of individual hiragana/kanji/katakana, i.e. word = (hiragana | kanji | katakana)+
* words have meanings
* kanji is special from hiragana and katakana, because:
  - kanji can have up to 3 pronunciations derived from chinese
  - kanji can have up to 2 pronunciations derived from japanese = 5 total possible pronunciations, same meaning for all. using these different pronunciations depends on the word in which it is used
* this means that a good learning strategy for kanji is to learn the most common reading, then learn the other readings when studying words
